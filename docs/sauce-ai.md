Sauce AI is the family of AI-powered capabilities across the Sauce Labs platform. It brings generative AI and AI-agent access to the tools you already use, so you can author tests in natural language, analyze results conversationally, explore application errors, and connect your own AI agents directly to the platform.

## Get Better Results with Prompting Guides

Every Sauce AI capability responds to natural language, and clear, specific prompts produce more accurate results. Use the prompting guide for the capability you are working with:

* **[AI Authoring Prompting Guide](/docs/sauce-ai/ai-authoring-prompting-guide.md)**: Describe user actions, request assertions, and use Data Variables when generating tests.
* **[AI Insights Prompting Guide](/docs/sauce-ai/ai-insights-prompting-guide.md)**: Ask focused questions about your test data.
* **[AI Error Reporting Prompting Guide](/docs/sauce-ai/prompting-guides/error-reporting.md)**: Query crashes and errors in plain language.
* **[Real Device Cloud Prompting Guide](/docs/sauce-ai/real-device-cloud-mcp-prompting-guide.md)**: Ready-to-use prompts for Sauce MCP.

## Privacy and Trust

Sauce AI is designed to minimize the data it sends to the model. Sauce AI for Insights mirrors the data access you already have elsewhere in the platform, and Sauce AI for Test Authoring never sends **[Data Variable](/docs/sauce-ai/test-authoring/data-variables.md#how-variable-values-are-protected)** values to the AI model. For details on how data is handled, see **[Privacy, Safety, and Trust](/docs/sauce-ai/ai-insights/privacy-safety-and-trust.md)** and the **[Responsible use of AI guidelines](https://trust.saucelabs.com/resources?s=tcd1vcgj9ya80ahtrhj65&name=responsible-usage-of-ai-at-sauce-labs)** in the **[Sauce Labs Trust Center](https://trust.saucelabs.com/)**.
