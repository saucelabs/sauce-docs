The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) was designed to harmonize data privacy laws across Europe, protect and empower all EU citizens' data privacy, and reshape how organizations across the region approach data privacy. Sauce Labs Mobile App Distribution is committed to providing a GDPR-compliant service and ensuring that its customers can use the platform in a manner that aligns with GDPR requirements.

For your convenience, we have compiled a list of frequently asked questions related to Sauce Labs Mobile App Distribution's GDPR compliance and data privacy practices. Below, you will find answers to common queries that may arise regarding the handling of personal data and the use of Sauce Labs Mobile App Distribution's services in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Can Sauce Labs Mobile App Distribution Provide a GDPR-Compliant Service?

Yes. Sauce Labs Mobile App Distribution can provide a GDPR-compliant service to customers on a Private Cloud plan. Contact us for more details.

## Can Sauce Labs Mobile App Distribution Provide Full Transparency?

Yes. Sauce Labs Mobile App Distribution end-users are ensured that their data is only used by Sauce Labs Mobile App Distribution for legitimate purposes, meaning for the purpose intended by the developers who use our platform. We do not and will never sell, transfer or otherwise harm your data, and we make sure it is only used via our services.

## Can Sauce Labs Mobile App Distribution Data Be Automatically Deleted After 30 Days?

Yes, for Private Cloud customers. The retention period is agreed as part of your Private Cloud setup and can be 30 days or any period your company requires; data older than that is deleted automatically. Deletion is permanent and can not be undone. On the shared platform, the retention period is defined by your subscription plan.

## Can the Service Be Hosted in Europe?

Yes. Private Clouds can be hosted in the US, Germany, or India.
