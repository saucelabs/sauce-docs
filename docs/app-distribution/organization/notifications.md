Stay informed about activity in your organization through in-app and email notifications.

## In-App Notifications

Click the **bell icon** in the top navigation bar to open the notification panel. Notifications appear for events like:

- New builds uploaded to projects you're assigned to
- Being added to or removed from a team
- Being added to or removed from a tester group
- Group assigned to a project
- Role changes

Unread notifications are shown with a badge count on the bell icon. Click a notification to navigate to the relevant page and mark it as read, or use **Mark all read** to clear them at once.

You can view all notifications with pagination by clicking **View All Notifications** at the bottom of the panel.

:::info
In-app notifications cannot be turned off — they are always active for all users.
:::

## Email Notifications

Email notifications are sent for:

- **Build uploads** — when a new build is uploaded to a project your group is assigned to.
- **App assignments** — when an admin manually sends a notification about a project to your group.

## Opting Out of Email Notifications

You can opt out of email notifications in two ways:

- **Profile settings:** Go to **My Profile** and toggle off **Email notifications**.
- **Unsubscribe link:** Click the **Unsubscribe** link at the bottom of any build or app assignment email.

Opting out only affects email notifications. In-app notifications (bell icon) are not affected and will continue to appear.

To re-enable email notifications, toggle the setting back on in your profile, or click the **Re-subscribe** link shown on the unsubscribe confirmation page.
