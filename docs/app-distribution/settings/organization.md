Manage your organization's identity and view your subscription. Go to **user menu → Organization Settings**.

:::info
Only **Account Owners** and **Org Admins** can access Organization Settings.
:::

## General

- **Organization Name** — the display name shown across the platform.
- **Subdomain** — your organization's unique subdomain (e.g., `mycompany.testfairy.com`). This field is read-only. To change your subdomain, contact support.

## Subscription Plan

View your current plan details:

- **Plan name** — your active subscription tier.
- **Apps Limit** — maximum number of projects allowed.
- **Testers Limit** — maximum number of testers allowed.
- **Data Retention** — how long builds and data are kept.

## Overview

A summary of your organization's current usage: number of teams, members, groups, and the creation date.
